About

Swarmtunes is a music app for all Neuro and Evil covers of songs, as well as the official releases and collabs. It features accounts, playlists and songs grouped by stream Unlike a cover drive this contains it's own UI and audio can be streamed without downloading Swarmtunes is fully supported on desktop and mostly on mobile. It also features PWA support which means it can be downloaded as a app (though not available in app stores!) I made this because I got sick of Spotify's terrible mp3 support. Also as an excuse to learn webdev Swarmtunes is a fan made site and is not associated with Vedal, Neuro-sama or SwarmFM
